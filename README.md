## What Is Urgent or Is It?

**Urgent or Is It?** is an educational cybersecurity awareness game focused on one of the most common manipulation techniques used in scams:

> **Urgency.**

The game challenges players to recognize suspicious situations, question pressure tactics, and avoid making decisions simply because a message demands immediate action.

Rather than presenting cybersecurity awareness as a purely theoretical topic, the project turns it into an interactive experience.

---

## The Core Idea

Scammers often try to prevent victims from thinking.

A message might claim:

- Your account will be closed.
- You must act immediately.
- Your payment has failed.
- You have won something.
- Someone needs your information urgently.
- You will face serious consequences if you do nothing.

The goal is to create enough pressure that the victim acts before verifying the situation.

**Urgent or Is It?** focuses on breaking that cycle.

```text
Urgency
   ↓
Emotional pressure
   ↓
"Act now!"
   ↓
Stop
   ↓
Question
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Step Back
```

# Gameplay

Urgent or Is It? is designed around cybersecurity awareness rather than technical exploitation.

Players encounter situations involving potentially suspicious communications and must determine whether the apparent urgency should be trusted.

The game encourages players to ask:

- Why am I being rushed?
- Who is asking me to do this?
- Is the request believable?
- What happens if I stop and verify?
- Can I confirm this through an official channel?
- Is the urgency itself being used as a manipulation tactic?

The objective is not simply to react quickly.

It is to learn **when not to react quickly**.

---

## Educational Goal

The central lesson of the game is:

> **Urgency is not proof that something is legitimate.**

A legitimate request can be urgent.

A scam can also be urgent.

The important skill is learning to recognize when urgency is being deliberately used to bypass careful thinking.

# Technology

Urgent or Is It? is a web-based cybersecurity awareness project.

The project uses:

- **TypeScript**
- **React**
- **NestJS**
- **MongoDB**
- **JWT authentication**
- **Local storage**

The application combines an interactive frontend with a backend API and database-backed functionality.

# Relationship to *Phish or Miss*

Urgent or Is It? is a **spiritual successor to *Phish or Miss***.

While *Phish or Miss* focused on phishing awareness, Urgent or Is It? expands the concept toward a broader psychological aspect of scams:

> **How urgency influences decision-making.**

The projects share the same educational spirit while approaching cybersecurity awareness from different angles.
